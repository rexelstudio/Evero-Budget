Privacy Policy

Your Data, Your Control

Effective Date: March 7, 2026

1 Information We Collect

To provide you with the best budgeting experience, we collect:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and encrypted password.
  • Financial Data: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and merchant names that you manually enter or sync.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and device information to help us troubleshoot and improve app performance.

2 How We Use Your Data

  • To maintain your account and provide core budgeting features.
  • To send you security alerts and account-related notifications.
  • To analyze aggregated (non-identifiable) usage trends to improve the app.

3 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal financial data to third parties. We only share data when:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services contractually obligated to keep your data private.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data is transmitted via SSL/TLS and sensitive fields are encrypted at rest.
  • Access Control: Only authorized systems access the database, with industry-standard password hashing.

5 Your Rights and Choices

  • Export: You can request a copy of your transaction data at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data through the settings menu. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered.

6 Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via your email address.
